Gur ka Thekua

Prep Time 20 mins
Cook Time 25 mins
Servings 6 people
Difficulty Medium

Gur ka Thekua is a traditional Indian sweet, especially popular during Chhath Puja. Made with jaggery, wheat flour, and aromatic spices, these crispy delights are deep-fried to golden perfection. The combination of jaggery's natural sweetness and warm spices creates an irresistible treat.

Ingredients

For the Dough

  • 2 cups Whole wheat flour
  • 1 cup Jaggery (gur), grated
  • 1/4 cup Ghee
  • 1/2 cup Milk
  • 1 tsp Fennel seeds, crushed
  • 1/2 tsp Cardamom powder
  • 1/4 tsp Black pepper powder
  • Pinch of salt

For Frying

  • Oil for deep frying

Optional Garnish

  • Sesame seeds
  • Chopped almonds

Instructions

  1. Heat ghee in a small pan and melt the grated jaggery with a little milk until it forms a thick syrup.
  2. In a large bowl, mix wheat flour, fennel seeds, cardamom powder, black pepper, and salt.
  3. Add the warm jaggery syrup to the flour mixture and mix well.
  4. Gradually add milk to form a stiff dough. The dough should not be too soft.
  5. Cover the dough and let it rest for 15-20 minutes.
  6. Roll the dough into small portions and shape them into round or oval discs about 1/4 inch thick.
  7. Using a fork or knife, create decorative patterns on the surface.
  8. Heat oil in a deep pan for frying on medium heat.
  9. Carefully slide the thekuas into the hot oil and fry until golden brown and crispy.
  10. Remove and drain on paper towels.
  11. Let them cool completely. Store in an airtight container.

Chef's Tips

  • Ensure jaggery is completely dissolved to avoid lumps
  • The dough should be stiff for crispy thekuas
  • Fry on medium heat for even cooking
  • Store in airtight containers for up to 2 weeks
