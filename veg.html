Besan Ki Sabji

Prep Time 15 mins
Cook Time 25 mins
Servings 4 people
Difficulty Easy

Besan ki Sabzi is a delicious and wholesome Indian dish made with gram flour, tempered with aromatic spices, and cooked to a creamy, velvety consistency. Its earthy flavors and rustic appeal make it a comforting choice, pairing beautifully with roti or rice for a satisfying homemade meal.

Ingredients

For Besan Ki Wadi

  • ½ cup Curd, beaten
  • ½ tsp Turmeric powder
  • ½ tsp Degi red chilli powder
  • A pinch of asafoetida
  • 1 cup Gram flour
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp Oil

For Curd Mixture

  • 2 cups Curd, beaten
  • 1 tsp Ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 Green chilli, chopped
  • 1 inch Ginger, chopped
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • ½ tsp Degi red chilli powder
  • A pinch of asafoetida
  • 1 ½ tbsp Coriander powder
  • 1 tsp Gram flour

For Gravy

  • 2 tsp Ghee
  • ¼ tsp Fenugreek seeds
  • ½ tsp Cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp Fennel seeds
  • 2-3 Dry red chillies
  • A pinch of asafoetida
  • 1 cup Water

For Tadka

  • 1 tsp Oil
  • ¼ tsp Carom seeds
  • ¼ tsp Onion seeds
  • ¼ tsp White sesame seeds
  • A pinch of degi red chilli powder

For Garnish

  • Fresh Mint leaves

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients for besan wadi and make a thick batter. Steam for 15 minutes until set.
  2. Cut the steamed wadi into cubes and set aside.
  3. Prepare the curd mixture by combining all ingredients and whisking until smooth.
  4. Heat ghee in a pan, add fenugreek, cumin, and fennel seeds.
  5. Add dry red chillies and asafoetida, then pour the curd mixture.
  6. Cook until it comes to a boil, then add the besan wadi cubes.
  7. Simmer for 10-12 minutes until the gravy thickens.
  8. Prepare tadka by heating oil and adding all tadka ingredients.
  9. Pour the tadka over the sabji and garnish with mint leaves.
  10. Serve hot with roti or rice.

Chef's Tips

  • Ensure the besan wadi is properly steamed to avoid raw taste
  • Whisk the curd mixture well to prevent curdling
  • Adjust spices according to your taste preference
  • Fresh mint leaves enhance the overall flavor
One Pot Meal

Tehri

Prep Time 20 mins
Cook Time 45 mins
Servings 6 people
Difficulty Medium

Tehri is a flavorful vegetarian biryani from Uttar Pradesh, made with basmati rice, mixed vegetables, and aromatic spices. This one-pot wonder combines the richness of traditional biryani with the wholesomeness of fresh vegetables, creating a perfect meal for any occasion.

Ingredients

For Rice

  • 2 cups Basmati rice
  • 4-5 Green cardamom
  • 2 Bay leaves
  • 1 inch Cinnamon stick
  • Salt to taste

For Vegetables & Spices

  • 1 cup Mixed vegetables (potato, peas, cauliflower)
  • 2 Large onions, sliced
  • 3 tbsp Ghee
  • 1 tsp Ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp Red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Garam masala
  • Fresh mint leaves
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • 2 cups Vegetable stock or water

Instructions

  1. Wash and soak basmati rice for 30 minutes, then drain.
  2. Heat ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot and fry sliced onions until golden brown.
  3. Remove half the onions and set aside for garnish.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste to remaining onions and sauté for 2 minutes.
  5. Add mixed vegetables and cook for 5-7 minutes until slightly tender.
  6. Add all spices and mix well. Cook for 2 minutes.
  7. Add the soaked rice and gently mix with vegetables.
  8. Add hot vegetable stock, whole spices, and salt.
  9. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and cover. Cook for 20-25 minutes.
  10. Let it rest for 10 minutes before opening the lid.
  11. Garnish with fried onions, mint, and coriander leaves.
  12. Serve hot with raita and pickle.

Chef's Tips

  • Use aged basmati rice for better texture and aroma
  • Don't over-mix the rice to prevent breaking
  • Cook on low heat to ensure even cooking
  • Let it rest after cooking for perfect texture
