Chicken Curry

Prep Time 20 mins
Cook Time 45 mins
Servings 4 people
Difficulty Medium

This authentic chicken curry is a perfect blend of aromatic spices and tender chicken pieces cooked to perfection. The rich, flavorful gravy pairs beautifully with rice or Indian bread, making it a comforting meal that brings the family together.

Ingredients

For Chicken Marinade

  • 1 kg Chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1 tsp Red chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt to taste

For Curry Base

  • 3 tbsp Cooking oil
  • 2 Bay leaves
  • 4-5 Green cardamom
  • 1 inch Cinnamon stick
  • 2 Large onions, finely chopped
  • 2 Tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tsp Coriander powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Garam masala
  • 1/2 cup Water or chicken stock
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken pieces with red chili powder, turmeric, ginger-garlic paste, and salt. Let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a heavy-bottomed pan. Add bay leaves, cardamom, and cinnamon.
  3. Add marinated chicken and cook until 70% done. Remove and set aside.
  4. In the same pan, add chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 2 minutes until fragrant.
  6. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they break down completely.
  7. Add all the ground spices and cook for 2-3 minutes.
  8. Return the chicken to the pan and mix well with the masala.
  9. Add water or stock, bring to a boil, then simmer covered for 15-20 minutes.
  10. Check seasoning and adjust salt and spices as needed.
  11. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot.

Chef's Tips

  • Marinating chicken for longer enhances the flavor
  • Cook onions until properly golden for rich taste
  • Adjust spice levels according to preference
  • Let the curry rest for 5 minutes before serving
